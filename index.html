About me

I am currently seeking PhD research internship opportunities. Please contact me at ignite@sjtu.edu.cn.

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Shanghai Innovation Institute (SII), supervised by Professor Junchi Yan.

My research focuses on quantum algorithms and machine learning, with applications to differential equations and quantum chemistry. My recent work includes quantum neural operators and neural operator learning.

I received my BSc in Physics from Zhiyuan College, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, in 2025.

Personal background

I am from Suqian, Jiangsu. I attended Huaiyin Middle School from 2016 to 2019 and joined the Jiangsu provincial Physics Olympiad team in 2018.
